## Interfaces implemented
`Recaller`, `Retriever` (x4), `Fuser`, `Scorer`, `ScoreGate`, `ObservationPreference`, `Packer`, `Includer`,
`TagFilterCompiler`. Consumed: `port.Reader`, `port.Embedder`, `port.Reranker`, `port.ModelRouter`,
`port.ConfigResolver`, `port.Clock`, `timex.TimeNormalizer`, `TokenCounter`, `Lookups` (optional).
