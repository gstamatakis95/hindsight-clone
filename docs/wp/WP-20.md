# WP-20 plan: worker, maintenance tick and cmd/memd hardening (final-review fix-up)

## Problems
- Final review B H3 + MEDIUM: the maintenance tick consolidates every bank with unconsolidated units. It
  ignores `auto_consolidation=false` and `enable_observations=false` (one no-op consolidate a minute, forever)
  and races `skip_consolidation` + manual Consolidate.
- Final review B MEDIUM: ReclaimStale and the shutdown release charge delete_bank attempts and FAIL it at 3,
  with no continuation, so a bank can stay tombstoned forever.
- Final review B LOWs: graph_dirty rows can wait for the next removal; a failing manual consolidate drops its
  retry when an automatic one is due; refresh/rederive/retag write no progress; retain Puts the blob before
  it checks the bank is live.
- Final review A (STATUS.md): duplicate MEMD_WORKER_ID defeats fencing; S3 keys unchecked; unbound
  EXTRACT/REFLECT only fail at the first call (as INVALID_ARGUMENT); no per-slot gateway concurrency env; no
  worker health endpoint; CreateBank on a deleting bank leaks SQL text; "."/".." ids accepted; health Watch
  semantics undocumented; the e2e bank delete does not check the blob prefix.

## Files
- `internal/adapter/postgres/maintenance.go` + `queries/maintenance.sql` (new): three Store reads consumed
  through worker interfaces (decisions 1452/1453 pattern): `UnconsolidatedBanks(ctx, before)`,
  `StalledBankDeletes(ctx)`, `BanksWithGraphWork(ctx)`. Tests in `maintenance_test.go`.
- `internal/worker/maintenance.go`: `ConsolidationCandidates.UnconsolidatedBanks(ctx, before)`;
  `MaintenanceDeps.Config` (port.ConfigResolver) and `ConsolidationGrace`; the consolidation step filters
  banks by effective config; new steps `resume_bank_deletes` (right after reclaim) and `graph_maintenance`.
- `internal/worker/exec.go`: the skip-retry rule applies to automatic consolidates only.
- `internal/worker/handlers.go`: `Handlers.Checkpoint`; refresh, rederive and retag report progress before
  they run (and stop there when cancelled or the bank is deleting).
- `internal/worker/control.go`: Cancel of delete_bank and Retry of another kind on a deleting bank are
  FAILED_PRECONDITION.
- `internal/engine/bank/banks.go`: CreateBank on a deleting bank is FAILED_PRECONDITION; an existing bank is
  a clean ALREADY_EXISTS (no SQL text).
- `internal/engine/retain/ingestor.go`: the pre-write read tx rejects a deleting bank before the blob Put.
- `internal/server/interceptors.go`: "." and ".." ids are INVALID_ARGUMENT. `health.go`: exported
  `NewHealthServer`, documented Watch semantics.
- `cmd/memd`: per-process worker id, S3 key validation, required EXTRACT/REFLECT, unbound-slot router
  wrapper, `MEMD_GATEWAY_MAX_CONCURRENT_<SLOT>`, `MEMD_WORKER_HEALTH_ADDR`,
  `MEMD_CONSOLIDATION_RECONCILE_GRACE`, wiring of the new tick dependencies.
- `test/e2e/scenarios_test.go`: BankDelete asserts a strict FAILED_PRECONDITION write and an empty blob prefix.
- `README.md` Status line; decisions 2000-2004.

No port, domain, proto or migration change. No CCR.
